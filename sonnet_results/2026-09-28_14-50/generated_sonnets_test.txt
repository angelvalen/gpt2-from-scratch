Two loves I have, of comfort and despair,
That like two spirits do suggest me still.
My better angel is a man right fair,
And stands to my right and left by the key;
My false one is a wit cruel
That never thinks but knows my fault.
One is better than the other, but at a loss,
Though I confide in the other all my thoughts.
O, I love, and hate alike; though I love,
No less then hate me, though I love better.
  Let me not depend on love, though it may be my true.
  Or, if love, though it be true, be thy servant,
  Thou art better than mine.
