Love is too young to know what conscience is;
Yet who knows not conscience is born of love?
Then, gentle cheater, urge not my amiss,
Nor compel my poor self-same love to justify.
For what could love's own true worth be
If it were not by truth and truth alone
The true of its own natural tellers?
And why should mine own tellers prove not true?
Because, sweet love, truth and truth alone are yours.
  But you have this power of your own self
  To prove your self false, though you claim no proof.
  Therefore, sweet love, praise your self so much
  That you might give some proof if you like,
  Even so that yourself might make others.

Two loves I have, of comfort and despair,
That like two spirits do suggest me still.
My better angel is a man right fair,
And I better love him than an earthly sight.
I love not man that best I know, for he
Is the invention of my sweet delight;
He is the livery of my thought-laden heart,
And I the cheerful presence of my longing mind.
  So my sweet angel stands by my side,
  And it is not by man's help I call,
  But by that sweet spirit which lives within.
  Then is my sweet angel in my breast.
  It is not by man's help that I call,
  But by that sweet spirit which lives within.
