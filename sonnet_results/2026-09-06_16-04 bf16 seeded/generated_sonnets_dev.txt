Thine eyes I love, and they, as pitying me,
Knowing thy heart torment me with disdain,
Have put on black, and loving mourners be,
Making others weep as they leap,
As though they themselves had died;
So, like the deformed swine, they take more,
And sad me with their rude abuse,
Making me more mourn than them if they were well.
Yet, that in all their crime I am,
They do me but honor, not hurt;
For they themselves love and love not what I hate,
That is, do what is to themselves end.
  But that in this love I love them more,
  Their death is the death of me, so do I die.
