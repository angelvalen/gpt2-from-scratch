Lo, as a careful huswife runs to catch
One of her feathered creatures broke away,
Sets down her babe, and makes all swift dispatch.
He says, then, "I shall not depart,"
And yet having gone thus far, he strikes
The head of a bird that went away before he;
He ends, "I shall not return;" yet more,
He lays hold on a pear, and gives it me,
And nothing, not even that much, is lost.
  Then might the passion of grief have been wont,
  So great that he seemed on the point to be back;
  Yet love having produced him he holds all,
  And bid me to go on.
