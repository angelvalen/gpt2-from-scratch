Thou blind fool, Love, what dost thou to mine eyes
That they behold and see not what they see?
They know what beauty is, see where it lies,
And by their own fancy shewn me do live,
Which is not true for their eye nor mine.
Truly I was the twain of lovers,
The shapes of two loving minds, and yet their eyes
Were in one, the friend that they loved,
And the subject of their love, the lover's.
Therefore was I one, the hated one,
The wisher of both, that two made me.
  And so, for in them dost thou thyself live.
