Lo, as a careful huswife runs to catch
One of her feathered creatures broke away,
Sets down her babe, and makes all swift dispatch.
As her wondrous babe, weary on his fast journey,
Loves his world, he turns back to earth, and never more.

And our love changes; our birds come back, and plants grow,

And dear love's beauty to-morrow wears itself out;
