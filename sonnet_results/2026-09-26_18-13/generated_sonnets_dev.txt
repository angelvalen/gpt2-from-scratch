So, now I have confessed that he is thine
And I myself am mortgaged to thy will,
Myself I'll forfeit, so that other mine may get a share.
But, on the other hand, I shall take a head,
To borrow some man's worth, or to trust him to sell me,
After thine hands must be redeemed.
With his brother I shall work the light's mark
And tarry with the fire's blaze,
And am for thy sake in merry times,
But thou hast fear'd of thy brother's death.
  I like to think of thee, but love me not,
  I love thee so dear that I can't love thee.

In faith, I do not love thee with mine eyes,

Love is my sin, and thy dear virtue hate,
Hate of my sin, grounded on sinful loving.
O, but with mine compare thou thine own state,
To understand the woman that bade me eat and drink,
And to say thy own love to me confess.
Yet such is my place, in thy heart's pleasure,
When I myself will you turn me to that day's grave,
And put on thy hard pity, that I may be to thee like my own.
  My love is to thee my liberty, thy most old love
  To thee the wild love of my soul.
