Two loves I have, of comfort and despair,
That like two spirits do suggest me still.
My better angel is a man right fair,
Whose love, like my own, I love more than death;
His worse, a woman more vile than any dam,
That, if I could but love her, I should be as mad as an earthen pot.
  But my better angel is not fair, and cannot be so;
  My better angel doth love me more than death,
  And, if I could but love him, I should be as happy as myself.
