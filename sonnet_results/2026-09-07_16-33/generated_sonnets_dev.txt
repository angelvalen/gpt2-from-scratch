Lo, as a careful huswife runs to catch
One of her feathered creatures broke away,
Sets down her babe, and makes all swift dispatch
Against that which returned the foe.
The same care, too, governs
The course of those that watch the ground;
They look for the first bud to appear,
And so have all this time kept their watch.
But now look again, and see if there be
A bud to be seen springing up from the weed;
Mine own was a bud that did bear a flower,
But that flower was blotted out by another,
And since that another did bear it again,
Mine own was but the second's counterpart;
  His was the flower, and his was the weed.
