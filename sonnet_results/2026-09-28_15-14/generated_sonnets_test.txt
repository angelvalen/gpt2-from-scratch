Those lips that Love's own hand did make
Breathed forth the sound that said "I hate"
To me that languished for her sake;
These were the fairest lips that ever breathed
Upon the face of man, and they loved me the more,
For they gave me their unblemished love while I lingered
In the fires of hell, where, if I die,
My sweet torturers shall taste of my excrement.
  Pity, pity, how I longed to die,
  When all the world loved me with such love as men give.

The beauty of his countenance she admired,
And in her heart she loved him still more than death.

The little love-god, lying once asleep,
Laid by his side his heart-inflaming brand,
Whilst many nymphs that vowed chaste life to keep,
With garlands of sweet-scented flowers doth fast pace,
Unconquered with all sensual delights,
Unconquered with all sensual delights, to show
How sweetly love doth delight in sensual delights,
Comes forth from his sepulcher to the field,
Comes forth to graze his golden lambs' feet,
Comes forth to drink the sweetest cup of life's nectar,
To show how sweetly love doth delight

When my love swears that she is made of truth,
I do believe her, though I know she lies,
That she might think me some untutored youth,
Whose love is but fair, whose heart is mean,
And whose tongue is not strong enough to say good word.
For love of him whose truth I love,
Who taught me to be self-controlled,
To keep silence when wrongs be done,
To give way when I am urged on,
To fear for my friend's life, even at the risk
Of treading on unfed ground.
  For fear of being thought a coward,
  To fly to some safe harbour where I may rest,
  Unharmed but weary from so hard a call.

Two loves I have, of comfort and despair,
That like two spirits do suggest me still.
My better angel is a man right fair,
Whose love, like yours, I perceive to be,
Not of this world only, but also of heaven.
For heaven is his mistress, and heaven his deliverer,
And heaven doth delight in his affections,
And heaven doth delight in your true love's part,
And heaven doth delight both in your good looks,
And heaven doth delight both in my poor state.
  And heaven doth delight both in my want of alms,
  And in my want of alms of any kind.
