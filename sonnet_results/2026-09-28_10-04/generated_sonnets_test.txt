When my love swears that she is made of truth,
I do believe her, though I know she lies,
That she might think me some untutored youth,
To keep her maid of this subject before
For which, to believe that she had chosen,

Two loves I have, of comfort and despair,
That like two spirits do suggest me still.
My better angel is a man right fair,
That the sight of me never, that the sight of you
Is always me fairer than my better angel;
And so I pray and go on your pleasure.
Yet ever I till your eye shall cease,
And both the good and the bad, that want a free enemy,
Thy friend and my friend, to whom I trust
In seeing you and speaking of your love,
That should my sweet love be found dead,
Then each of them at once would be worthier.
  So though what makes a best friend love may be
