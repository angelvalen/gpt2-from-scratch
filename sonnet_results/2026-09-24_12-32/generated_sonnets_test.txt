The little love-god, lying once asleep,
Laid by his side his heart-inflaming brand,
Whilst many nymphs that vowed chaste life to keep,
Were they chaste to their own tongue and pen,
Loving to lay their sweet foot upon it.
Thus when love's sweet heart was in store,
The vulgar doe was new love's first veil,
And the first of all women was he.
Thus, then, he was called the darling of love,
And like his darling this first rose was made.
  So is love's first love ever true,
  Not being in love and yet loving the world.
