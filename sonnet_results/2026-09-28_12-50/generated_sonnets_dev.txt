In faith, I do not love thee with mine eyes,
For they in thee a thousand errors note;
But 'tis my heart that loves what they despise,
And what I most despise is no true apology,
Which I am most impious in love to have.
Then the wise man says, 'Thou art my love, and so well gave,
That thou art the fairest, the most beautiful,
And so worthy of thine,
That I may offer thee glory in that which I love,
In whom I love to be truth's child,
