My love is as a fever, longing still
For that which longer nurseth the disease,
Feeding on that which doth preserve the ill,
Or, by that prodigal motive, taking that dead,
That to him all homage and love is more
Than that which through loving hearts doth dwell
The death of those who loved and died.
This love is most noble, but in the third world
By changing the chief thing in the glory
Of love doth keep his love alive;
Therefore the repugnance of love doth hang
Within the end of winter's day.
  This, that is the kiss I offer to your love,
  Is more than that which does be changed.
