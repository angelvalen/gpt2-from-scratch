Two loves I have, of comfort and despair,
That like two spirits do suggest me still.
My better angel is a man right fair,
Who in gentle apparel doth keep pace with the times,
Serving, too, alack for alack the common dish,
That double pleasure of body and mind may sing together.
He hath a mind of his own, a tongue of his own praise,
Which in sweet discourse doth impart cheer and pleasure to mind.
'Tis him that hath taught me to love two others,
That I may have three, that I may have four.
